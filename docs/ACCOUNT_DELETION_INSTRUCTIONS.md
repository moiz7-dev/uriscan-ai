## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: Can I recover my account after deletion?
A: No, account deletion is permanent and irreversible. All data is permanently removed.

### Q: How long does deletion take?
A: Deletion is processed within 30 days of your request. You will receive a confirmation email when complete.

### Q: What if I change my mind?
A: Once deletion is processed, it cannot be reversed. If you change your mind before processing, contact us immediately.

### Q: Will my data be shared before deletion?
A: No, your data is not shared with third parties during the deletion process.

### Q: Can I delete just my data but keep my account?
A: Currently, we only offer full account deletion. Contact support if you have specific needs.

### Q: What happens to my purchases?
A: All purchase records are deleted. You will lose access to any premium features.

### Q: Do I need to uninstall the app?
A: Yes, you should uninstall the app after deletion to remove any local data from your device.
