## 4. Data Storage and Security

### 4.1 Data Storage
- **Local Storage:** Some data is stored locally on your device using SQLite and AsyncStorage
- **Cloud Storage:** Account data and scan history are stored securely on Supabase servers
- **Image Processing:** Camera images are processed locally whenever possible to minimize data transmission

### 4.2 Security Measures
We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:
- **Encryption:** Data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- **Access Controls:** Strict access controls limit who can access your data
- **Secure Authentication:** We use secure authentication methods including OAuth
- **Regular Audits:** We regularly review our security practices

### 4.3 Data Retention
- **Active Accounts:** We retain your data while your account is active
- **Deleted Accounts:** Upon account deletion, we permanently delete your data within 30 days
- **Anonymous Data:** Some anonymized usage data may be retained for analytics purposes
